# Founding records to prepare — draft v0.3

19 October 2026. Templates for Nigerian counsel and the founding association.
Nothing below records an actual appointment, incorporation, vote or asset transfer.
Do not commit private identity evidence or completed sensitive application data.

## Schedule 1 checklist

Complete the public-safe Schedule 1 in the constitution and a separately secured
CAC application annex with only the particulars legally required. Obtain advice
on access, privacy notices and retention. Full names, qualifications/residence,
consent and declarations of all three founder trustees must be checked; do not
invent names, dates, signatures, votes or residence eligibility.

## Founding association meeting record

Record the actual meeting date, place/method, notice, eligible legal/founding
members, quorum, independent advice, agenda and votes. Draft resolutions for
lawful consideration:

1. Establish the association under its approved name and nonprofit objects;
   record initial legal members and their equal rights and responsibilities.
2. Adopt the legally reviewed constitution, citing its exact version and schedules,
   with effective-date and CAC-approval conditions explained by counsel. Adopt
   the constitution’s non-distribution and dissolution provisions expressly.
3. Appoint **three identified founders** as the initial trustees, with their consent,
   responsibilities, qualification evidence and fixed three-year founding tenure
   proposed from incorporation. Record that activation of representative elections
   will not trigger their immediate re-election, but fair recall/removal remains.
4. Appoint the required Chairperson and Secretary, record powers, acceptance and
   responsibility for minutes, membership records, terms and compliance.
5. Authorise the named applicant to complete lawful name reservation, application,
   notices, declarations, payment of filing charges and responses to CAC queries.
   This does not authorise automated filings from the platform.
6. Approve the initial-body powers and proposed banking framework subject to bank
   requirements, constitution and counsel; separately adopt exact signatories and
   expenditure limits before organisational funds flow.
7. Record conflict declarations and resolve the separation of trustee oversight
   from any paid operational role under section 838 before compensation is offered.
8. Approve the transition preparation plan, independent oversight, disclosures and
   asset inventory without asserting that assets have already been transferred.

Attach actual signed minutes showing attendance and votes, signed consents and
applicable declarations in the appropriate private legal records. Counsel must
settle the final resolutions and required filing evidence under ss825–827.

## On incorporation and succession

- Obtain and verify the certificate, approved name and registered constitution.
- Record incorporation and initial three-year term dates, common expiry and the
  180/90-day election preparation deadlines in Schedule 1 and the term register.
- Keep representative activation dates distinct from trustee dates. Keep the
  actual legal-member register distinct from accounts and online activation.
- For elected renewals/replacements, record independent results and disputes,
  actual formal meeting notice, quorum, signed resolution and counsel’s filing
  determination. Retain CAC correspondence, written assent where required and
  the effective-date/legal-authority handover record. Same-person re-election is
  not automatically a fresh appointment filing or automatically exempt.
- Prepare asset assignments/vesting and access changes with title, licence and
  contractual consents, shared recovery and independent evidence. No residual
  unilateral founder control over essential assets after transfer.
- Publish only milestones supported by evidence, and limitations on any temporary
  authority. No online result or template creates statutory trustee status.
