# Supporting governance policies — draft v0.3

19 October 2026. Proposed operational policies implementing the constitution,
not an independent source of legal powers. All require Nigerian legal review.
No voting, identity, payment or appointment functionality is enabled.

## Membership and verification

Constitutional dependency: Articles 2–5 and 6 must recognise admission, rights,
voluntary activation, restrictions and formal meeting participation.

- Keep a private legal-member register with documented application, constitution
  accepted, admission decision/date, reasons, appeal and lawful cessation. A
  platform account or NIN check is not admission. Secretary decides under published
  criteria within 30 days; independent admission appeal can be brought within 14
  days. No founder may reserve a permanent voting class.
- Every eligible legal member may voluntarily activate or deactivate platform
  voting. Deactivation does not terminate legal membership; legal resignation is
  separate express notice. Equal formal meeting rights survive online opt-out.
- Preserve 18+ verified voting age, identity review and uniqueness, 30-day account
  age and 14-day ballot freeze for admission, verification and activation. Publish
  eligibility checks, accessible routes and sanctions with independent appeal;
  criticism is not a disqualification. All votes have equal weight.
- NINAuth remains the initial preferred, disabled design in
  [identity-verification.md](identity-verification.md). Future lawful equivalent
  methods need published legal/privacy/security review and effective duplicate
  controls across providers. No raw NIN or sensitive evidence may be public.
- Separate restricted identity, legal admission, voting activation, eligibility,
  optional public badge and secret ballots. Stop online eligibility on revoked or
  expired checks without automatically terminating legal membership. Preserve
  lawful records under reviewed retention rules and permit correction/appeal.

## Elections and disputes

Constitutional dependency: Articles 7–9 and 13 must recognise each office, term,
selection, recall, vacancy and legal follow-through. Policies cannot change terms.

| Contest | Seats | Term / first regular election |
|---|---|---|
| Registered trustees | 3 together; no staggering | Founders’ initial three years proposed from incorporation; first community election at expiry, then every three years |
| Community representatives | 2 together | One year; begin when readiness gates are satisfied, then annually |

The founding term is not re-contested at community activation. Founders have no
renewal entitlement or preferential nomination; they may contest equally. Retain
two consecutive full terms per office category followed by one full term out;
founding term counts. A vacancy replacement serves only the unexpired remainder.

1. Publish the calendar, roles, impartial candidate qualifications (including
   statutory/CAC trustee qualifications), nomination and appeals route, candidate
   conflicts, campaign rules and independent election officers before nominations.
   Establish equal access without founder approval. Prepare trustee elections 180
   days before expiry; publish the timetable by 90 days before expiry.
2. Use separate contests. A voter can select up to three candidates for a normal
   trustee election, up to two for representatives, once per candidate and on one
   equal-weight secret ballot per contest. For vacancies use the number being
   filled. Highest-supported candidates win; runoff resolves a tie affecting seats.
3. Keep 14-day ordinary consultation, 14-day eligibility cutoff and seven-day
   ballot. Preserve existing proposal endorsement and turnout rules, including at
   least 100 eligible verified opted-in adult legal members before binding online
   ballots. Failed turnout means no decision and a rerun, not quietly lower quorum.
4. Independently audit eligibility and aggregate counts without a convenient
   identity-to-choice mapping. Publish totals and review results with appropriate
   redactions. No public identity evidence, identifiable raw ballots or voter list.
5. An independent panel accepts disputes within 14 days of results, hears affected
   parties fairly and gives reasons; material disputes must be resolved before
   certification and reruns ordered where necessary.
6. Keep association meeting notices, attendance and signed resolutions separate
   from online evidence. A trustee election/recall alone does not change statutory
   status. Obtain legal advice and required CAC assent; record legal and democratic
   term dates accurately. Annual representative elections ordinarily need no
   trustee replacement filing; check separate officer/particulars events.

## Moderation independence and recall

Constitutional dependency: Articles 6, 9 and 11 reserve individual moderation and
private identity matters outside public voting and recognise independent review.

- Moderators apply published rules with reasons and appeals; governance ballots
  never decide a named person’s post, ban, identity or private report.
- Separate governance sanctions from ordinary moderation. A disputed electoral
  restriction goes to an independent adjudicator or recused panel. Publish only
  aggregate/anonymised accountability information.
- A 10% active-electorate petition starts online recall; a separate 10% eligible
  legal-member requisition permits formal recall under Article 6 without online
  opt-in. Give notice, grounds, a fair response and independent oversight. Use
  majority approval and the ordinary quorum of the applicable route. No founder
  permission is necessary; statutory removal/filing safeguards remain mandatory.
- Emergency safety action receives independent or Council review within seven
  days where safe and lawful. Administrative suspension never pretends to be a
  court/CAC order or automatically changes the trustee register.

## Conflicts, finance and independence

Constitutional dependency: Articles 1, 7, 10, 12 and 14 must establish non-distribution,
legal finance powers, recusals, banking, asset controls and dissolution restrictions.

- Keep annual and transaction-specific conflict declarations, connected-person
  interests, recusals and disinterested minutes. A conflicted person does not count
  toward the affected quorum. No disinterested quorum: obtain lawful formal member
  decision and independent advice; members cannot authorise prohibited payments.
- No private shareholder, donor, political party or commercial sponsor controls
  votes or receives profits. Review donations, grants, voluntary contributions,
  subscriptions, revenue and sponsorship conditions for lawful objects and tax
  treatment. Publish material conditions; reject capture or political dependence.
- Proposed bank mandate: three or more authorised signatories, two independent
  approvals per transfer or withdrawal, separate initiation and approval and no
  unilateral mandate changes. Trustees approve exact arrangements with the bank
  and constitution. Record expenditure limits before collecting funds; budget
  exceptions require minuted approval and reserved member consent where applicable.
- Founder operational work can be legitimate, but governing-body salary, fee,
  rent or benefit requires written s838 legal clearance and disinterested approval.
  Recusal is not a legal exemption. Volunteer oversight and separately employed
  non-council founders are possible options subject to counsel; do not create
  artificial ex-officio roles or disguise salaries as consultancy fees.
- Maintain accounts, reconciliations and an asset register; budget annually, publish
  quarterly actual treasury reports and arrange independent annual audit plus
  statutory accounts/returns. No fake balances. Private donor, personnel, identity
  and banking data must be redacted. See [legal-review.md](legal-review.md) for
  separate annual, bi-annual, appointment, particulars and amendment filings.
- Asset transfers need title/licence/contract checks, executed documents and access
  handover with shared recovery. No founder retains unilateral essential access.
  Residual assets on lawful dissolution go to eligible compatible institutions or
  the lawful charitable destination after debts, never private members or founders.

## Readiness and trustee succession

Constitutional dependency: Articles 7–9 and 13 expressly authorise founding terms,
initial-body powers and the lawful, time-limited fallback. Counsel must approve
these provisions before incorporation; a policy cannot create holdover authority.

- Before incorporation the operator/founding association only has its documented
  existing authority. After incorporation the initial three-trustee body has
  quorum two, majority non-conflicted decisions and no casting vote. Full Council
  has three trustees plus two elected representatives, quorum three and the same
  decision rule. No founder-only veto; no representative becomes a trustee by default.
- Require every gate in [open-items.md](open-items.md) before platform activation:
  adopted legal framework, separate lawful admission, voluntary activation,
  provider/privacy/security readiness, sufficient electorate and independent
  ballot oversight. Representative elections do not reset founding trustee terms.
- At vacancies (resignation, death, established incapacity, disqualification,
  lawful early removal or expiry), notify members within seven days, begin the
  replacement process within 30 days and aim to complete lawful selection within
  90 days. Fill the remainder, preserve common cycles and complete legal resolutions
  and CAC procedures. A runner-up route is allowed only if disclosed beforehand
  and lawful; there is no unilateral founder co-option.
- For founding expiry, prepare 180 days before and publish the calendar by 90 days
  before. If the platform is unavailable, independently administer a lawful formal
  general-meeting selection by expiry under the constitution, preserving equal
  legal-member participation. It is not a live online ballot and cannot bypass
  the online electorate/security gates. Disclose that limitation.
- If selection cannot be completed by expiry, any caretaker authority requires a
  documented legal basis and independently reviewed member resolution before expiry;
  only essential operations and succession, maximum 90 days, no renewal under the
  fallback clause. It does not extend democratic tenure. If authority/quorum is
  missing, seek counsel and appropriate CAC/court directions, not self-extension.
- Pending approval is not authority for an unapproved replacement or an expired,
  resigned or disqualified trustee. Publish legal basis, limits, end dates and
  outstanding procedures. Seek lawful directions if filing or other delays extend
  beyond the authorised period; preserve assets without inventing legal powers.
- Track the seven transition phases separately. Final community-control claims
  require legally effective elected trustee leadership, completed essential asset
  control, final transition report and independent evidence, with dependencies
  disclosed. Keep regular elections and accountability thereafter.
