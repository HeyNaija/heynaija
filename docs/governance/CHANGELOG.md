# Governance draft history

## Privacy policy — 30 October 2026 — published

- `/privacy#rights`: members can delete their account in Settings. It leaves
  HeyNaija at once and is erased after 14 days, during which they can sign in
  and keep it. Other requests by email are answered within 30 days. It used to
  say deletion was not in the app yet.
- `/privacy#keep`: what happens to an account when it is erased and what is
  kept (a blank placeholder, reports and moderation decisions, posts kept as
  moderation evidence, the record of an 18+ declaration, the record of the
  request), that backups of both databases age out within 30 days, and that
  copies of sign-in emails are kept 7 days. Periods counsel must set stay as
  placeholders.
- `/privacy#cookies`: `hn_deletion`, which holds the erase date for an hour
  after someone asks.
- Not yet reviewed by counsel: what a member is told is entry C-15 in the
  [counsel log](counsel-log.md).

## v0.3 — 19 October 2026 — proposed; not adopted

- Select nonprofit Incorporated Trustees under Part F of CAMA 2020 as the
  preferred structure, replacing the open cooperative-first choice. Preserve
  independent operation, no private shareholders, no profit distributions,
  nonprofit asset protections and legally compliant dissolution.
- Separate platform users, formally admitted legal members and voluntarily
  activated voting members. Every eligible legal member has equal access;
  online opt-out never itself ends legal membership or formal meeting rights.
- Specify three founders as initial trustees, appointed through lawful founding
  association procedures and included in the CAC incorporation application.
  Propose initial three-year terms from incorporation, subject to counsel review.
- Exempt the founding term from immediate community-wide trustee re-election
  when governance activates; retain recall, fair removal and statutory safeguards.
- Elect all three trustee seats together at initial term expiry and every three
  years thereafter; no staggering, founder preference or automatic renewal.
  The previous published v0.2 proposed five annual council seats, not a staggered
  trustee system; this revision expressly rules out staggering going forward.
- Introduce two annually elected non-trustee representatives from governance
  activation, producing the intended five-member Council alongside the trustees.
- Retain the existing two-consecutive-full-term limit and one-term break for each
  office category; the founding trustee term counts. Different term lengths
  therefore give trustees and representatives different maximum continuous tenure.
- Separate the initial three-trustee body from the full Council, with quorums two
  and three respectively, majority decisions, no casting vote, conflict recusals
  and no new founder veto. Formal meeting rules are proposed for legal review.
- Preserve online decision thresholds, 100-person readiness, secret auditable
  ballots, independent oversight, recall, disputes, emergency review within seven
  days, age safeguards, privacy and moderation independence. State separate
  three-seat and two-seat election methods and legal appointment dependencies.
- Permit future legally acceptable identity alternatives with equivalent age and
  uniqueness safeguards. NIN remains initially preferred; verification stays off.
- Add a seven-phase transition distinguishing incorporation, legal membership,
  elected representation, elected trustee governance, legal asset transfer and
  final independent confirmation. Propose election preparation 180 days before
  expiry, timetable by 90 days before expiry, and a legally validated formal
  meeting fallback with no automatic founder holdover. Any authorised caretaker
  arrangement is limited to 90 days and non-renewable under that clause.
- Clarify distinct CAC returns, renewals, replacements, particulars and amendment
  events. Flag section 838 paid-office restrictions; require legal clearance,
  disinterested approval, financial disclosure and multiple bank signatories.
- Separate the CAC constitution from the Community Governance Charter and
  supporting policies; generate the public and CAC texts from one source.
- Add a fail-closed legal admission requirement to preparatory voting rules;
  document the missing register instead of building a live appointment system.
  All startup feature locks remain. No payments, filings or elections activated.
