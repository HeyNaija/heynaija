# Nigerian legal review — draft v0.3

19 October 2026. This is a source-backed issue register for qualified Nigerian
nonprofit counsel, **not approval to adopt, file or operate the arrangement**.
Every constitutional clause and operational policy needs review as a whole.
All fixed periods and extra internal thresholds below are HeyNaija proposals
unless expressly attributed to a legal source.

## Statutory anchors and matters to validate

| CAMA anchor | Legal distinction / review question |
|---|---|
| ss825–827 | Application evidence includes signed appointment minutes; constitution addresses trustee tenure, replacement, meetings and governance. Validate three founders, qualifications, term start and all required particulars. |
| s834 | Replacement/addition requires general-meeting resolution and CAC approval. Written assent gives validity from the appointment resolution. Counsel must settle interim authority, removal and handover records. |
| ss836–837 | A governing council includes trustees and exercises assigned functions. Validate three-plus-two composition, initial body, quorum, reserved matters and delegation. |
| s838 | Non-distribution and restricted governing-body remuneration. Review paid-office prohibition, ex-officio wording and limited permitted benefits; recusal alone is insufficient. |
| s833 | Constitutional alterations require lawful member resolution and CAC approval. Validate stricter internal thresholds and publication procedures. |
| ss845–848 | Statements, accounts, retention and annual returns are separate compliance duties. |
| s850 | Court dissolution and nonprofit residual-asset destination; a platform vote alone does not dissolve the body. |

CAMA specifies constitutional tenure provisions; this research does **not**
establish that HeyNaija’s precise three-year founding arrangement, no-immediate-
re-election rule or fallback is automatically acceptable. Counsel must expressly
approve and document them, including all three founders’ eligibility.

The CAC checklist, p61, says non-residents cannot be trustees. Confirm its current
legal basis and application to the proposed founders and diaspora candidates.
Do not convert this trustee-office restriction into unequal voting rights for
otherwise eligible legal members. Verify statutory disqualifications under s826
and current documentary requirements before nominations or incorporation.

## Distinct filing events — do not infer one from another

| Event | Required treatment before implementation |
|---|---|
| Annual return | Separate recurring duty under s848 / Form CAC IT4, with audited accounts and required member/council particulars. It is not a replacement appointment application. |
| Bi-annual statement | Separate s845 / IT5 duty. Regulation 32 specifies statements to 30 June and 31 December, filed by 15 July and 15 January following. Confirm current requirements. |
| Trustee re-election, same person | Record the member decision and next term. Counsel must determine if a change, fresh appointment or other filing is required on these facts. Neither a blanket filing exemption nor automatic fresh application is asserted. |
| Replacement or additional trustee | s834 general-meeting resolution, application, required notice/publication and objection process, qualification evidence and written CAC assent; IT3 in the published checklist. Counsel records the legally effective date and permitted actions pending assent. |
| Changed trustee particulars | Separate supporting evidence and particulars-change process, even if no election occurs. |
| Annual representative election | Not ordinarily a trustee replacement; update internal Council records and applicable returns. Check any separate officer/particulars filing if relevant. |
| Chairperson or Secretary change | Check the distinct officer-change process, even when the person is not a new trustee. |
| Constitutional amendment | s833 / IT7 process with required resolutions, notices and approval, distinct from annual returns and trustee changes. |

Regulation 37 sets a 15-day filing period for post-incorporation applications,
except annual returns, bi-annual statements and miscellaneous filings; applications
requiring publication are not processed until the 28-day publication period ends.
Build a counsel-reviewed timetable that satisfies both; **do not wait for the
objection period to finish before checking the filing deadline**. The checklist
separately lists minutes, declarations, identity evidence and newspaper notices
for replacements. Confirm current form names and portal evidence rather than
hard-coding an automatic filing workflow.

Regulations 33–36 specify the January–December financial year, applicable account
standards, membership register and minute books. Section 848’s annual-return
window and accounting period must be translated by counsel/accountants into an
actual filing calendar; quarterly public reports do not replace statutory filings.
The 2022 checklist contains inconsistent section labels in places (e.g. annual
returns and constitution alteration); use the Act, not a checklist typo, for the
legal basis, and confirm current administrative practice.

## Questions requiring written professional clearance

1. Confirm association formation, approved objects/name, three founder trustee
   qualifications (including residence), signatures, declarations, initial officers
   and a lawful application resolution under ss825–827. Complete Schedule 1.
2. Approve initial fixed three-year tenure from incorporation and protection from
   immediate re-election at activation, subject to recall/removal; approve a common
   three-year cycle and two-consecutive-full-term cap including the founding term.
3. Settle admission and cessation of legal membership, impartial admission appeals,
   protected formal meeting rights, member requisitions and independent convening
   without founder veto. Validate proposed 21-day notice, quorum and voting rules.
4. Validate secret online ballots, independent certification, electronic/hybrid
   meetings and any proxy arrangements. Do not apply Part B company provisions
   to Part F without a legal basis. Community approval is distinct from legal
   association resolutions; the constitution must give delegated decisions effect.
5. Validate general-meeting recall/removal, resignation, death, incapacity,
   disqualification, replacement and temporary-vacancy authority. Settle s834’s
   approval/retrospective-validity wording without authorising unapproved action.
6. Validate election preparation at least 180 days before expiry and timetable by
   90 days before expiry; lawful formal-member fallback if the online system is
   unavailable; any strictly non-renewable 90-day caretaker authority. There is no
   automatic holdover. Obtain appropriate directions if legal capacity is absent.
7. Review s838’s paid-office and benefit restrictions for **both trustees and
   representatives**, including the ex-officio exception and expenses, rent and
   reasonable service fees. No blanket permission for founder salary or consultancy.
   Prefer volunteer oversight separated from independently supervised lawful paid
   operational roles outside the governing body; a trustee may need lawful
   resignation/replacement before taking an incompatible paid office.
8. Approve banking, fundraising, contributions, sponsorship independence, permissible
   platform revenue, tax treatment, expenditure limits, accounts/audit/retention,
   conflicts and connected-party procurement. Nonprofit status is not tax clearance.
9. Review domain/IP title, contributor and software licences, provider contracts,
   assignments, infrastructure and fund vesting, cross-border obligations and shared
   recovery. Confirm s850 residual beneficiaries and necessary court procedures.
10. Retain all privacy, NINAuth, NDPA, youth and security gates in open-items.md.
    Approve equivalent lawful identity alternatives without weakening age, privacy,
    uniqueness or equal member access.

## Constitution / charter reconciliation

No intentional substantive conflict remains: each uses three fixed-term trustees,
two annual representatives, voluntary equal-access legal-member voting, the same
term limits, seven phases and legal follow-through. The charter retains current
service promises; its future governance provisions remain proposals. The legal
constitution controls after lawful registration; policies require constitutional
recognition for admission, voting authority, recall, succession, funds and asset
controls. Counsel must reconcile the **final adopted texts**, including the still
unadopted charter notice period and all schedules. A published draft is not proof
of legal effectiveness or completed asset control.
