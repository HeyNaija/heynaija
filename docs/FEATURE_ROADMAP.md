Audit scope: the local staging working tree and its inspected code, tests and operational documentation. Staging and production are deployed, but this audit does not verify their live revisions or individual feature availability there, except where a feature is recorded in an environment. This tracks product development, not formal governance decisions. Priority means importance, not completion. No delivery dates or launch-readiness percentages are implied. The public readable counterpart is `/progress`; the repository is private, so the website does not link to it.

## Status and availability

- **Implemented** (`implemented`): Working behaviour is supported by application code and tests. Deployment is tracked separately.
- **Partial** (`partial`): A working subset exists, with remaining work or verification stated.
- **Planned** (`planned`): Proposed work without a working implementation of the described feature.
- **Blocked** (`blocked`): Progress needs an external approval, decision or prerequisite.

- **Recorded in production** (`production`): the owner confirmed the feature working in production; this does not show which revision is deployed there.
- **Recorded on staging** (`staging`): staging is recorded in deployment documentation; it is not production or proof this local revision is deployed.
- **Not live** (`not_live`): the described feature is unavailable.
- **Availability not verified** (`unknown`): code and test evidence do not establish live availability.

#### Guided new-member onboarding

- **ID:** `member-onboarding`
- **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** Implemented · **Availability:** Not live
- **Description:** Help newcomers choose interests, communities and people to follow.
- **Inspected evidence:** `packages/db/drizzle/0019_onboarding.sql`, `apps/api/src/onboarding/onboarding.service.ts`, `apps/api/src/onboarding/onboarding.controller.ts`, `apps/web/app/(main)/welcome/page.tsx`, `apps/web/app/(main)/page.tsx`, `apps/web/lib/onboarding.ts`, `apps/api/src/onboarding/onboarding.service.test.ts`, `apps/web/tests/onboarding.test.mjs`, `e2e/tests/onboarding.spec.ts`, `e2e/tests/auth.spec.ts`
- **Audit notes:** New members are sent from the home page to /welcome until they finish or skip it (members.onboarded_at; migration 0019 set it for everyone who joined before). Step 1 chooses communities, step 2 suggests up to 12 people (moderators and recent posters in the chosen communities, then recent posters site-wide; never yourself, suspended members, blocks either way, or people already followed or muted), and finishing joins and follows everything in one transaction through the usual join and follow code, so counts, follow notifications and the shared action rate limit apply; it lands on Following with a nudge to post. Every step, and the whole flow, can be skipped. Communities stand in for interests until topic-following exists; there is no interest or topic picker. No inviter is suggested: invites are admin-created codes. The forms are plain forms, but the page streams like other signed-in pages, so the known no-JavaScript limitation applies. The data export (format 7) includes onboardedAt.

The kinds decide what notifies a member at all, so they decide what is emailed too; email itself is one switch beside them (email-notifications). The data export (format 7) includes the choices.

The end-to-end tests use a stand-in push service; delivery through the real push services, and how the notifications look on Android, iOS and desktop, have not been checked on staging.

## Maintaining this registry

Schema version 1 is validated in `apps/web/lib/roadmap.ts`, shared by the generator and server-rendered page. Every feature requires a stable kebab-case ID, name, one-sentence description, known category, P0–P3 priority, lifecycle status, availability and inspected evidence paths. Partial and blocked entries require a separate public caveat. Implemented entries require both application and test evidence; a migration or placeholder alone does not qualify. Dependencies must refer to other feature IDs and must not form cycles.

Evidence paths and operator notes appear only in this developer document. Public caveats must be safe to publish and must not reveal internal security details. Do not add private repository URLs to the public page. Record a verification date only with an actual verification record; do not manufacture a latest-update date when generating.

The local audit does not attest to the live staging revision, production launch, provider approval, legal adoption or completion of an operational rehearsal. Confirm those independently before changing availability or removing a caveat.
