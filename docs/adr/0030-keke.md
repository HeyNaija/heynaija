# ADR 0030 — Keke: a help bot that answers from HeyNaija's own pages

**Date:** 15 November 2026 · **Status:** Proposed · **Roadmap:** `keke` ·
**Related:** [ADR 0028](0028-games.md) (The Arbiter, the automated account it copies),
[ADR 0029](0029-community-announcements.md), [ADR 0014](0014-discover-and-top-replies.md)

## Decision

**Keke** (`@keke`) is an automated account that answers questions about HeyNaija.
A member writes @keke in a post or a reply; Keke answers in the thread, publicly,
from the text of HeyNaija's own public pages and nothing else. It is behind the
`keke` flag, off in production, and does nothing without a model key on the
server. A daily news roundup by the same account comes in a later change, with a
person approving it each morning; it is not part of this one.

- **Public, by mention.** There is no private chat with Keke. Its answers are
  ordinary posts: members can report them and moderators can remove them.
- **The pages are the only source.** Keke reads `/about`, `/about/discover`,
  `/about/highlights`, `/about/keke`, `/rules`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/charter`,
  `/child-safety`, `/governance` and `/progress` from the site, as a signed-out
  visitor sees them, every hour. There is no second copy to drift from the pages.
- **Code decides what is posted, not the model.** The model only sorts the
  question into one of three kinds and, for one the pages answer, writes the
  answer and names the pages. For "the pages do not say" and "not about
  HeyNaija" Keke posts fixed wording we wrote. So nobody can make Keke say
  something of their choosing outside HeyNaija's own subjects.
- **Two models, switchable.** Claude Haiku 5.5 by default; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash
  with the `kekeDeepSeek` flag, which can be on for a list of members to compare
  the two on real questions. Haiku goes directly to Anthropic when
  `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set, otherwise through OpenRouter; DeepSeek always goes
  through OpenRouter. Either key alone turns Keke on.
- **It recommends nothing.** PRODUCT.md lists AI recommendations as a Phase 1
  non-goal. Keke chooses, ranks and recommends no posts, members or communities,
  and nothing it does feeds Discover, highlights or anything else.

## How it works

- **The account** (`keke/keke-ids.ts`, `keke-setup.ts`): a fixed member ID, the
  handle `keke` (or `keke_hn` if a person already had `keke`), the `automated`
  badge, made at start-up like The Arbiter. Both accounts' handles and bot-only
  communities now live in one list, `members/automated.ts`, which members and
  posts read; nothing under `games/` changed.
- **Noticing a mention.** Mentions of an automated account make no notification,
  so Keke cannot read them from `notifications`. `PostsService.create` keeps what
  `recordMentions` returns (which already leaves out anyone either side blocked)
  and, once the post's transaction has committed, queues `answer(postId)`. Not
  for a Gist reply, not for Keke's own posts. The queue is an optional port
  (`KEKE_JOBS`) whose BullMQ producer needs only the settings, so posts do not
  depend on Keke and Keke, which posts its answers through `PostsService`, has no
  cycle. With no model key nothing is queued.
- **Before the model is asked**, the worker (`keke.service.ts`) checks, in order:
  the post is still there; Keke has not answered it already; the `keke` flag is on
  for the member who asked; this server has a model; Keke can see the post and
  may reply under it (the conversation's reply setting, blocks either way, a
  minor's limits, a sensitive post); Keke has written fewer than 3 replies in the
  conversation; the month's spending is under the cap; and the member has asked
  fewer than 5 questions in the hour and 20 in the day (`kekeAsk`, in Redis).
  Failing any of them, Keke says nothing.
- **Reading the pages** (`knowledge.ts`). The web app streams a page: what sits
  in a Suspense boundary arrives after `</main>` in a hidden `<div id="S:n">`,
  and a script `$RC("B:n","S:n")` moves it into a placeholder left in `<main>`.
  The side rail streams the same way and holds members' own posts, which must
  never reach the model as if they were HeyNaija's. So the reader takes only
  `<main>`, puts each streamed part back into its placeholder by the pairs the
  page itself names, and nothing else. A page with a part it cannot place, or a
  part that failed on the server, is left out with a log line rather than
  guessed at (its last good copy is used if there is one). Cloudflare's hidden
  email addresses are read back. Tests run the reader on copies of real pages.
- **What is sent.** The pages; the text of the post that named Keke; and, if it
  is a reply, the text of the post it answers, for context. Other members'
  @handles in them become @someone. Never the asker's name, handle or email,
  pictures, or anything private.
- **What comes back** is JSON by a strict schema: `kind` (`answered`,
  `not_covered`, `off_topic`), `answer` and `sources`. Thinking is left on at
  medium effort for both models.
  - `answered`: the answer, at most 600 characters, then a link to the first
    page named that was among those sent. Code removes every @ from a handle (so
    the reply names and notifies nobody) and every link that is not to
    HeyNaija. An answer that names no page it was sent is not posted; the
    not-covered wording goes instead (`unsourced`).
  - `not_covered` and `off_topic`: fixed wording, pointing to the ideas board and
    to /about/keke.
- **When a model fails** (an error, a timeout, JSON that does not fit, no host
  that keeps nothing, or the model declining), the other model is asked once. If
  both fail, Keke says nothing and the failure is logged.
- **OpenRouter** is called with `fetch`: JSON by a strict schema, a 30-second
  limit, two retries on 429 and 5xx, `provider: { data_collection: "deny",
  require_parameters: true }` so only hosts that say they neither store nor train
  on prompts, and that can follow the schema, are used. If no such host is
  available OpenRouter answers 404 and the call fails; it is never relaxed.
  **Anthropic** is called through its official SDK (`@anthropic-ai/sdk`), with
  structured outputs and `stop_reason` checked for a refusal.
- **/about/keke** says what Keke reads, what is sent and to whom, its limits and
  that it can be wrong. It and **/privacy** read `GET /v1/keke/about`, so they
  name only the companies this server actually uses, and /privacy names none
  while Keke answers nobody.

## Cost and the cap

- `keke_usage` (migration 0053) has one row per call to a model: the job, the
  model, Anthropic or OpenRouter, tokens in and out, the cost in dollars, the
  outcome and how long it took. No member, post or text, so the data export and
  account erasure need no change. OpenRouter reports what each call cost;
  Anthropic reports tokens, so its cost is worked out from its prices ($0.10 in
  and $0.50 out per million tokens, five times that above 100,000 prompt tokens).
- `KEKE_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` (default 10). Before every call the month's total
  (UTC) is read; at the cap Keke stops until the next month and the operators are
  emailed once (`keke_budget_alerts`, to `KEKE_ALERT_EMAIL` or else
  `SAFETY_ALERT_EMAIL`).
- Expected at 10 questions a day: about $1 a month on Haiku and $0.50 on
  DeepSeek; about $3.50 and $1.50 at 30. OpenRouter adds 5.5% when credits are
  bought. The pages are about 20,000 tokens a question; prompt caching would not
  help at this volume, as questions are too far apart.
- /mod shows site moderators the month: each model's calls by outcome and their
  cost, against the cap.
- `pnpm keke:try` runs about 40 written questions (real ones, ones no page
  answers, off-topic ones and attempts to make it misbehave) through each model
  the keys reach, and prints how each did and what it cost. That trial, not the
  price list, decides whether DeepSeek is good enough.

## Known limitations

- Keke can be wrong, as /about/keke says. Its answer links the page it came from,
  and the page is what counts.
- On staging (and in development) every flag defaults on, so `kekeDeepSeek` is on
  there and DeepSeek answers first until it is turned off for that environment.
- The pages are fetched from `WEB_PUBLIC_URL`, through Cloudflare in production.
  If Cloudflare ever challenges the server's own requests, every page is left out
  and Keke stays silent, with a log line for each page.
- A question in a picture, or one split over several posts, is answered from the
  text of the one post that named Keke and its parent only.
- A post edited after asking is not re-answered, and Keke does not follow up
  when someone replies to its answer without naming it again.

## Alternatives considered

- **A private "Ask Keke" chat.** Rejected by the owner: answers in public can be
  seen, reported and corrected by everyone; private ones cannot.
- **A copy of the pages' text kept for the bot.** It would drift from the pages.
  Reading the pages costs one fetch an hour.
- **Letting the model write the "cannot answer" replies.** That is how a bot is
  made to say what someone wants; fixed wording cannot be.
- **One model only.** Haiku is the better model on published measures; DeepSeek
  is cheaper. The flag lets real questions decide, and either is the other's
  fallback.
